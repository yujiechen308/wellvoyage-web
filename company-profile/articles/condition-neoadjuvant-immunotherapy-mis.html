Neoadjuvant Immunotherapy + Minimally Invasive Surgery

新辅助免疫+微创手术
← Back to Where China Leads

For locally advanced (stage II–III) lung cancer, Chinese oncology teams helped define the modern sequence: 2–4 cycles of PD-1 immunotherapy (with or without chemotherapy) to shrink the tumour first, then a minimally invasive resection on a down-staged, easier-to-operate field. China's domestic PD-1 agents made this protocol affordable as well as effective.

What it treats & who qualifies

  • Resectable stage II–III non-small cell lung cancer where downstaging improves the operative field.
  • Tumours with high PD-L1 expression or other biomarkers predicting immunotherapy response.
  • Medically fit patients able to commit to a 3–5 month treatment window.
  • Not for: oncogene-addicted cancers (EGFR/ALK driven) where targeted therapy — also strong in China — replaces immunotherapy.

Why patients travel to China for this

  • China ran and published some of the landmark neoadjuvant immunotherapy trials in lung cancer; major pathological response rates reported by Chinese series are at the international frontier.
  • Domestic PD-1 inhibitors (camrelizumab, sintilimab, tislelizumab, toripalimab) cost a fraction of Western list prices — often US$2,000–5,000 per cycle self-pay versus US$12,000–15,000 in the US.
  • The complete pathway — medical oncology, surgery, pathology — is delivered inside one hospital system, avoiding the fragmented handovers that break this protocol elsewhere.

The procedure, step by step

  1. Biopsy and PD-L1 testing confirm diagnosis; PET-CT and brain MRI complete staging.
  2. 2–4 cycles of PD-1 (± chemotherapy) run on a 3-week cadence, with CT response assessment after cycle 2.
  3. Once re-staging shows resectable disease, surgery is scheduled — single-port or multi-port VATS depending on anatomy.
  4. Post-operative pathology (MPR assessment) guides adjuvant therapy decisions.
  5. Adjuvant immunotherapy continues up to a year, coordinated with your home oncologist.

Recovery, week by week

  • Weeks 1–9: Cycles 1–3 on a 21-day cadence; response CT after cycle 2; most side effects manageable as an outpatient.
  • Week 10–12: Re-staging, surgical booking, minimally invasive resection.
  • Week 13–17: Hospital stay 4–6 days, then recovery; pathology review sets the adjuvant plan.
  • Month 4–12: Adjuvant immunotherapy up to a year, largely manageable at home with the Chinese records.
