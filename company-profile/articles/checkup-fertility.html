What's included4 module groups

Her side

  • Anti-Müllerian hormone (AMH) and full hormone panel — FSH, LH, estradiol, prolactin
  • Antral follicle count on transvaginal ultrasound
  • Uterine cavity assessment, with hysteroscopy if indicated
  • Thyroid function, glucose metabolism and karyotype if indicated

His side

  • Semen analysis including morphology and DNA fragmentation
  • Hormone panel — testosterone, FSH, LH, prolactin
  • Scrotal ultrasound where clinically indicated
  • Karyotype and Y-chromosome microdeletion testing if indicated

Shared

  • Carrier screening for common recessive conditions
  • Infectious disease screening required before any assisted cycle
  • Reproductive endocrinology consultation for both partners together

Output

  • English report with a written assessment of realistic options — IVF, IUI, or expectant management
  • Where relevant, a planned protocol outline and timeline

Who this is designed for

  • Couples trying for six months or more (three months if over 35)
  • Women considering egg freezing who want an honest ovarian-reserve picture first
  • Couples with a previous failed IVF cycle seeking a second opinion
  • Anyone with a family history of early menopause or recurrent pregnancy loss

Why guests do this in China

  • The diagnostics come first. A single cycle of tests determines whether IVF is even the right answer, which is often not the case at the first consultation.
  • Both partners in two days. Semen analysis, hormone panels and imaging for a couple run in parallel rather than across months of separate appointments.
  • Protocol based on your numbers. You leave with a written assessment of realistic options and, where relevant, a proposed protocol — not a sales pitch for the most expensive route.

How it works, day by day

  1. Day 1 morning — Her hormone panel and AMH, antral follicle count, uterine assessment; his semen analysis.
  2. Day 1 afternoon — His hormone panel and ultrasound; carrier and infectious disease screening for both.
  3. Day 2 morning — Joint reproductive endocrinology consultation with a written options assessment and, if appropriate, a protocol outline.

Frequently asked questions

What does a checkup in China cost?

We quote on request. Chinese hospitals set self-pay rates individually depending on the modules selected and the hospital tier, so a published figure would be misleading for most guests. Send us the package you are interested in and we return a written, itemised quote within 24 hours — hospital fees, English report, interpreter and transfers all listed separately, before you book anything.

How long do the results take?

Hormone results are usually same-day, semen analysis within 24 hours, and karyotype or carrier screening within about two weeks. Where tests run past your departure we send them to you directly.

Is this assessment suitable if we have not decided on treatment?

That is exactly who it is designed for. Many couples complete an assessment and then wait a year, with the numbers to make the decision properly when the time comes.

Can we do this as a single person?

Yes. Egg-freezing candidates in particular come alone, and the assessment adapts to one partner.
