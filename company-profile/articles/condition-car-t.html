Your own T cells are collected, genetically reprogrammed to recognise your cancer, multiplied, and re-infused — a living drug that has driven complete remissions in leukaemias and lymphomas that had run out of options. China approved multiple domestic CAR-T products (the world's second-largest approved arsenal) at prices of roughly a tenth of US list prices, and runs one of the world's largest CAR-T clinical trial portfolios.

What it treats & who qualifies

  • Relapsed or refractory B-cell malignancies: diffuse large B-cell lymphoma, follicular lymphoma, ALL.
  • Patients whose disease has failed two or more prior lines, or after transplant failure.
  • Medically fit for lymphodepletion; organ function and infection screening passed.
  • Other cancers through clinical-trial screening — the eligibility review is free and honest.

Why patients travel to China for this

  • China has multiple domestically approved CAR-T products (NMPA, 2021 onward) — with domestic pricing at roughly US$45,000–85,000 versus US$370,000–530,000 list prices abroad.
  • China runs among the largest CAR-T clinical trial portfolios in the world — including targets beyond CD19 and registries that enrol patients whose commercial options are exhausted.
  • Treatment is delivered inside fully credentialed transplant-and-cell-therapy units with 24/7 cytokine-release management.

The procedure, step by step

  1. Eligibility review: pathology, prior-therapy records, organ function, disease burden assessment.
  2. Lymphodepleting chemotherapy prepares the immune niche (hospital admission).
  3. Your CAR-T product (manufactured earlier from your collected cells, or an approved off-the-shelf-matched product) is infused — over minutes.
  4. Days 1–14 are monitored in hospital for cytokine release syndrome and neurotoxicity, with tocilizumab and steroids on standby.
  5. Response assessment at day 28; long-term follow-up tracks persistence and late effects.

Frequently asked questions

What are the risks?

Cytokine release syndrome and neurotoxicity are the signature risks — fever, hypotension, confusion — which is why treatment happens inside experienced transplant units with round-the-clock management. Published Chinese series report safety profiles comparable to international registries.

Do I qualify if my cancer is not a lymphoma or leukaemia?

Approved products today target B-cell malignancies; for other cancers the realistic path is often a clinical trial — and China's CAR-T trial portfolio is among the broadest anywhere. WellVoyage screens your records against open-protocol eligibility.
