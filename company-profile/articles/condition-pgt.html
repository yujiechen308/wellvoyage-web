PGT — Preimplantation Genetic Testing

第三代试管（胚胎植入前遗传学检测）
← Back to Where China Leads

Before an embryo is transferred, a few cells are biopsied and screened — for chromosome number (PGT-A), for a known familial mutation (PGT-M), or for structural rearrangements (PGT-SR). China's PGT programmes are licensed and large-scale, and for families carrying hereditary disease, PGT-M means having a healthy child without rolling genetic dice.

What it treats & who qualifies

  • Couples both carrying recessive mutations (thalassaemia, cystic fibrosis, sickle-cell).
  • Known dominant familial diseases (Huntington's, Marfan's, familial cancer syndromes).
  • Chromosomal rearrangement carriers (translocations, inversions) with recurrent loss.
  • Advanced maternal age or recurrent aneuploid loss — PGT-A to lower miscarriage risk.
  • Previous child affected by a testable genetic condition.

Why patients travel to China for this

  • China's licensed PGT programmes cover all three modalities (PGT-A / PGT-M / PGT-SR) with large published cohorts and rapid laboratory turnaround times.
  • PGT-M for monogenic disease (thalassaemia, haemophilia, Huntington's, BRCA-related syndromes) is delivered with genetic counselling in both Chinese and English.
  • PGT-A adds roughly US$1,500–3,000 to a cycle in China, versus US$4,000–7,000 in the US.

The procedure, step by step

  1. Genetic counselling reviews your family history and previous testing; a testing strategy is agreed.
  2. IVF cycle proceeds to blastocyst stage (day 5–6); a 5-cell trophectoderm biopsy is taken.
  3. Embryos are vitrified while the biopsy is analysed (array CGH, NGS, or mutation-specific testing).
  4. Results arrive in 1–3 weeks: affected, carrier, unaffected, or aneuploid status per embryo.
  5. A euploid / unaffected embryo is transferred in a frozen cycle; the rest remain stored.

  • Lab window: Results in 1–3 weeks; report categorises every embryo.

  • Pregnancy: Standard prenatal care at home with the genetic report in your file; prenatal confirmation testing remains routine.

Frequently asked questions

Does PGT guarantee a healthy baby?

It dramatically lowers — not eliminates — the tested risks. PGT-A reduces miscarriage from aneuploidy; PGT-M prevents the tested familial disease. Prenatal testing in pregnancy remains standard practice, and the Chinese counselling sessions say so explicitly.

We carry a rare mutation — can you still test for it?

Usually yes: PGT-M test design (haplotyping) works for rare variants, and Chinese PGT labs handle unusual cases routinely. Send your family's genetic reports — the lab's feasibility answer comes back before you pay anything.
